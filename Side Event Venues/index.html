    How the Eventability Score works

    Every venue is rated 0–5 on ten factors: capacity, accessibility, event infrastructure, booking flexibility, atmosphere, networking potential, food & beverage, operating hours, location relative to the IBW/Devcon ecosystem, and value. The score is the average across all ten factors, doubled to a 10-point scale. Factors we could not assess are shown as unknown in the breakdown and counted as a neutral 3.0 in the total — so unknown is never treated as bad, but a venue can't rank higher simply because less is known about it.

    Confidence reflects how much sourced evidence sits behind a venue: high means published capacities or official event specs, medium means official listings plus consistent guest reports, low means limited public evidence — call before you plan.

    Sources are shown under each venue. Coordinates and addresses come from Google Maps; capacities from venue websites, Cvent, and event-listing platforms; formats from published patterns of TOKEN2049 side events, mapped to Mumbai equivalents. Nothing is fabricated. If you know a number we're missing, tell us.
