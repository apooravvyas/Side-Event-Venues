How SIDEQUEST decides

Eventability Score

Every venue is rated 0–5 on ten factors — capacity, accessibility, event infrastructure, booking flexibility, atmosphere, networking potential, food & beverage, operating hours, location relative to IBW/Devcon, and value — then averaged and doubled to a 10-point scale. Factors we could not assess show as unknown and count as a neutral 3.0: unknown is never treated as bad, and a venue can't rank higher just because less is known about it. Venues assessed on fewer than 8 factors display with a tilde (~8.1).

Confidence

High means published capacities or official event specs plus three or more independent evidence points. Medium means official listings and consistent guest reports. Low means limited public evidence — call before you plan. Capacity ranges marked estimated are our reading of the venue's scale, not a published figure.

Fit to your brief

The shortlist starts from Eventability, then adjusts for what you told it: whether the venue is built for your format, whether your headcount sits inside its capacity range, how far it is from the anchor you picked, and the single factor that matters most for that format — networking for a mixer, operating hours for an afterparty, infrastructure for a workshop, food for a dinner. A venue that fails on size or format is marked a stretch and listed last, never hidden.
